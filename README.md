# Hospital Emergency Room Analysis (Excel)

## Summary

In this project, I worked on analyzing hospital emergency room data to understand patient flow, wait times, and overall efficiency of the system. The goal was to identify where delays were happening and how the hospital could improve its operations.

Using Excel, I created a dashboard that tracks key metrics and highlights patterns in patient visits, demographics, and department referrals.



## Objective

* Analyze patient wait times and identify delays

* Understand patient volume and flow

* Study patient demographics

* Identify which departments receive the most referrals



## Dataset

The dataset consists of 500+ patient records with details such as:

* Patient Age

* Gender

* Wait Time

* Department Referred

* Admission Status

* Visit Details



## Approach

* Cleaned and structured the dataset in Excel

* Used Pivot Tables and formulas to analyze data

* Created a dashboard to visualize key metrics and trends



## Key Insights

* Around 62% of patients experienced delays, indicating inefficiency in emergency response.

* The 0–9 age group had the highest number of visits, which was unexpected.

* Patient distribution was fairly balanced across genders.

* General Practice and Orthopedics received the highest number of referrals.

* About 52% of patients were admitted, showing moderate admission rates.



## Key Metrics Tracked

* Patient Wait Time

* Total Patient Count

* Admission Rate

* Department-wise Referrals



## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Formulas)
